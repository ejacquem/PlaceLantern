# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- The lantern can now also be placed on the vertical item frame (config: `AllowOnVerticalFrame`).
- The lantern's light now casts shadows so thin walls block it (configs: `CastShadows`, `RangeMultiplier`).
- Fixed the lantern not showing after re-entering the world.

## 1.0.0
- Initial release: lantern can be placed on the horizontal item frame.
